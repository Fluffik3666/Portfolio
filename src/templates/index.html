i'm Sasha.

I'm a developer, photographer, and founder based in London. I'm the CEO of KidsHustle — a platform helping kids and teens earn money through quick jobs.

I build things for the web and Apple platforms, with a stack spanning Python, Swift, and modern web technologies backed by Google Cloud and Kubernetes. I care about clean architecture, developer experience, and shipping things that work.

I'm also part of Young Founders of London — a curated community of the city's most ambitious student entrepreneurs.

Outside of code, I do theatre tech, love cooking, and I'm usually out with a camera somewhere in London.
